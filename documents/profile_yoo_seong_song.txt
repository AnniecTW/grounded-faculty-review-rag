BIOGRAPHY

Yoo-Seong Song is an associate professor at the School of Information Sciences and also holds an appointment at the University Library. Before joining academia, he earned an MBA from the University of Iowa and an MSI from the University of Michigan.

Prior to joining the University of Illinois, he worked as a senior associate at Ernst & Young, specializing in e-commerce, high technology, and telecommunications consulting. He also worked at Accenture as a consultant and at Tellabs as a market intelligence analyst.

As an entrepreneur, he co-founded two information service firms in Korea. He currently collaborates with domestic and international corporations to provide students with real-world consulting projects through experiential learning courses.
