BIOGRAPHY

Jingrui He joined the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2012. Her research focuses on heterogeneous machine learning, active learning, neural networks, and self-supervised learning, with applications in sustainability, agriculture, social network analysis, healthcare, and finance.

She has received numerous awards and recognitions for research contributions in machine learning and data mining, including NSF CAREER, IBM Faculty Awards, and ACM Distinguished Member recognition.
