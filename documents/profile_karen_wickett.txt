Research focuses on information organization, metadata, and knowledge organization systems. Her work examines the conceptual and logical foundations of information systems, data curation, and methods for organizing and representing information.

BIOGRAPHY

Karen Wickett is an associate professor in the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Her research explores the conceptual and logical foundations of information organization systems and artifacts.

She is particularly interested in information systems involving documents, datasets, databases, digital objects, metadata records, and collections.

Before joining the University of Illinois, Wickett served on the faculty of the School of Information at the University of Texas at Austin, where she taught and conducted research on information modeling, conceptual modeling, and data curation. She also completed postdoctoral research at the Center for Informatics Research in Science and Scholarship (CIRSS).
