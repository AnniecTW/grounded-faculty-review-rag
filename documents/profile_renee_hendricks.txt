BIOGRAPHY

Renee Hendricks joined the School of Information Sciences in 2022.

Before joining the University of Illinois, she held multiple technical and leadership positions within U.S. federal agencies, including:

- Systems Engineer, Transportation Security Administration (TSA)
- Biometrics Analyst, Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
- Test and Evaluation Branch Chief, U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)
- Research and Development Manager, Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)
- Research Fellow, U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)

Prior to her federal government service, Hendricks was a faculty member at Purdue University, where she taught in the aeronautical program and conducted research related to biometrics and aviation security.
