TITLE: Record Number of Instructors Ranked as Excellent
ORGANIZATION: School of Information Sciences, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
DATE: September 9, 2025

SUMMARY

Fifty-seven iSchool instructors were named to the University of Illinois Teachers Ranked as Excellent list for Spring 2025, the highest number ever recognized for the School.

The rankings are based on student evaluations collected through the Instructor and Course Evaluation System (ICES). Only instructors who administered ICES evaluations and consented to publication were included.
