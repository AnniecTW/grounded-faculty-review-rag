SUMMARY

Student reviews generally describe Madelyn Sanfilippo as a caring, approachable, and supportive professor who provides helpful feedback and reasonable grading. Many students appreciate her willingness to help and her flexible assignment structure. However, some reviewers find the lectures boring or overly focused on reading slides, and several note that the course relies heavily on readings and memorization.
